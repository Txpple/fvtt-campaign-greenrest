## Rules & rulings made at the table (now precedent)

- **Advantage/disadvantage cancel = straight roll, and stays canceled** — "once something
  gets canceled out it stays that way; otherwise it's too confusing with stacking." (Vow of
  Enmity + displacement → normal roll.)
- **Nick mastery order:** the DAGGER (the Nick weapon) triggers Nick; the extra attack folds
  into the Attack action and **frees the bonus action** — it does NOT grant a third attack.
  Jetten accepted and immediately spent the freed bonus on Healing Word. This is the standing
  reading.
- **Bonus-action leveled spell locks the action to cantrips — INCLUDING wand activations.**
  DM looked it up mid-fight; Gren's same-turn Healing Word + Wand volley was let slide once
  as already-rolled, then enforced.
- **Administering/drinking a potion = bonus action "for now"** — flagged as "some tables say
  action; we'll deal with that another day." House default: bonus action.
- **Precision Attack: attack roll only, never damage** — re-taught twice tonight (Anthony,
  then confirmed when the DM proxied his roll). Third session it's come up; consider a card.
- **Spellfire Burst rider** confirmed in play: any ≥1 sorcery-point spend on the turn
  (Subtle Spell qualifies) → Bolstering Flames temp HP to a chosen creature. Used twice.
- **"All healing doubled" house-rule question (open since session 2): RESOLVED — there is no
  house rule.** Tom's "healing is doubling / so much better" table comment referred to the
  2024 rules baseline (Cure Wounds 2d8, Healing Word 2d4). All tonight's heals were rolled
  RAW 2024. Close the session-2 flag.
- **Cover:** quarter cover through fog run as −2 to hit (attacker-side penalty, consistent
  with the session-2 convention).
- **Weapon mastery = no daily training bookkeeping** — "if you're wielding the weapon, use
  the mastery." (Said while Morgash shopped for a pike/greataxe.)
- **Lantern of Revealing in combat:** treated as a Magic action to unhood; 30-ft bright light
  strips displacement entirely while the beast is in the light (they disengaged to the edge
  and re-cloaked outside it). Matches the plot doc's "light anchors the wound" intent —
  now table-canon.
