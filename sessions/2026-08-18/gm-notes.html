1 · Where the campaign now stands

Shards: 1 / 4. The party holds The Graveheart, in the party stash (not attuned, no attunement needed). This is the first milestone — level-up to 5 is due.

They are still underground. The session ended in room 05 (Hollow's Heart) immediately after the vision. Stated intent: leave and get back to camp. Session 5 has to resolve the walk out — decide now whether the Hollow restocks (the DM raised the question in play and left it open).

Selma never happened. The party went straight from the Kestrel camp to the shrine and never re-entered Greenrest. The Selma scene, the potion restock, and Warin's half-cashed barracks whisper all carry forward untouched. The potion economy is now genuinely dry — this matters.

Reveal-dream timing ⚠️

Per the plot doc, a reveal dream OPENS a session, never closes one, and the party has exactly the prerequisite now (1 shard). Session 5 must open cold with Appendix A.1 — "she is asleep · warmth" — before shopping, before the recap, before anything. Dress it in the day's trace: a warm red stone in a dead hand that will not open.

One wrinkle: they will be sleeping rough in the forest or in a dungeon, not in Greenrest. Decide whether A.1 fires wherever they sleep (recommended — the shard is the trigger now, not the town) or waits for a bed. Also note nobody drank tonic tonight, so nothing blocks it.

The gray tree

Per §5.3/§5.4, one shard held should show the gray tree its first green in living memory, and the square should talk of nothing else. The party is not in town to see it — so the town discovers it first. That is a gift: they walk back into a Greenrest that is already buzzing about something they caused and don't know they caused.

New tonight

4 · Rules and table issues to fix

  • Combat automation was a clear win. Multiple unprompted compliments; combat moved much faster than session 3. The DM has "a bunch of notes for things to fix" — capture them.
  • Bugs seen in play:
    • Shield Master's bash offered itself against a creature that was already dead.
    • Topple prompted on an already-prone target.
    • Weapon-mastery Vex/Slow prompts fire but nothing applies the benefit — the player must claim it in the roll dialog, and everyone forgot at least once.
    • Gren's Wand of the War Mage +1 did not appear to be applying its +1 to spell attack rolls even when equipped and attuned; Tom re-equipped and re-attuned with no change. Investigate.
    • Innate Sorcery's save-DC bump did not show in effects; Tom had to re-target himself.
    • A stray "map artifact" token appeared in room 03 and had to be explained away.
    • The combat carousel position drew complaints — the DM wants a Baldur's-Gate-style single row.
  • Rules clarified at the table (worth a player-patch-notes line): weapon mastery is free and stacks multiple masteries per turn; Bless should go on the attackers and on the caster (for concentration); Divine Smite is a bonus action in 2024 and cannot ride a crit; smite and Lay on Hands compete for the same bonus action; sorcery points return on a short rest (half sorcerer level, once per long rest); hit dice return at half maximum on a long rest.
  • Long-rest-in-a-dungeon ruling was made on the fly and was the right call. Write it down.
