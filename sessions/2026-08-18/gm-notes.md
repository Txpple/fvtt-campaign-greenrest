Per the plot doc, **a reveal dream OPENS a session, never closes one**, and the party has exactly the
prerequisite now (1 shard). **Session 5 must open cold with Appendix A.1 — "she is asleep · warmth"**
— before shopping, before the recap, before anything. Dress it in the day's trace: *a warm red stone
in a dead hand that will not open.*

One wrinkle: **they will be sleeping rough in the forest or in a dungeon**, not in Greenrest. Decide
whether A.1 fires wherever they sleep (recommended — the shard is the trigger now, not the town) or
waits for a bed. Also note **nobody drank tonic tonight**, so nothing blocks it.

### The gray tree
Per §5.3/§5.4, one shard held should show the gray tree **its first green in living memory**, and the
square should talk of nothing else. The party is not in town to see it — so the *town* discovers it
first. That is a gift: they walk back into a Greenrest that is already buzzing about something they
caused and don't know they caused.

## 3 · Loose threads

**New tonight**
1. **The Graveheart's vision is the only bridge from this site to her.** It fired correctly and the
   party read it correctly. They have not yet said "the tree in the square" out loud in connection
   with it — but they will. Let them.
2. **"One chamber of a heart — and a heart has four."** This hint has *not* been delivered yet. Hold
   it for when someone examines the shard properly (the item description carries it).
3. ⚠️ **The shard tax was not recited at the lift.** The planted payload — *crystal · obviously still
   a heart · warm · it beats · it never takes a mark* — did not get said when Jetten picked it up;
   the vision carried the moment instead. **Hesper delivered two thirds of it early** in her origin
   story (*"warm, beating, the size of two cupped hands"*), which is arguably better placement. Say
   the full four beats next time anyone handles it, and at every subsequent shard.
4. **Hesper killed the party's Greenrest theory without them noticing.** She dated herself at
   300–500 years and never mentioned the town — but **nobody asked her the word "Greenrest."** The
   planned kill-shot beat (*"I don't know that word"*) did not fire. It is still available: the
   lockets, the Necrotic Swords, or simple reflection can bring them back to it. Do not force it.
5. **Nobody has connected "three greedy treasure hunters found a warm beating shard in a cave" to
   "there are three more shards out there."** When they do, the four ruins stop being a menu and
   start being a countdown.
6. **The faceless, handless statue** appeared twice (rooms 01 and 05) and Thomas correctly read it as
   pre-theological and self-invented. He asked *"would it be pre-human?"* and got a partial answer.
   This is good, load-bearing texture — the shrine genuinely has nothing to do with the Dreamer, and
   the party has now confirmed that with their own checks.
7. **Gren's Everseed Acorn** got its first real airing — *"I still have not found the place where I
   belong."* Greenrest is the obvious answer the campaign is dangling. Note that the finale kills
   the valley. That is a knife worth keeping sharp.

   and only Hesper made wights, from living captives — but the party doesn't know that and the
   tension was excellent. Consider never correcting it directly.

   session to a Monday. **He is fine for Tuesday 25 August.** Sort this by email.

  - The combat carousel position drew complaints — the DM wants a Baldur's-Gate-style single row.

  stacks multiple masteries per turn; Bless should go on the attackers and on the caster (for
  concentration); Divine Smite is a bonus action in 2024 and cannot ride a crit; smite and Lay on
  Hands compete for the same bonus action; sorcery points return on a short rest (half sorcerer
  level, once per long rest); hit dice return at half maximum on a long rest.
