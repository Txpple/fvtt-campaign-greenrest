# Session 7 — GM Notes · Story (2026-09-08)

_Plot: what changed in the world tonight, what was promised, what is now canon, and what is
left hanging. Nothing here about the system or the table — that is `gm-notes-mechanics.md`._

---

## 1 · What changed tonight

- **Three of four hearts.** Graveheart (Morgash), Tideheart (Gren, attuned overnight in
  Greenrest), **Duskheart** (Jetten, taken off Harrow's chest and attuned on the spot). The
  Sisters' Notes said *GREEN, BLACK — not yet found*; black is found. **Green is the last piece
  and nobody has a location for it.**
- **Renlow Veck is dead** — executed in a cell, chained and unarmed, by Thomas, over Morgash's
  arm. Morgash then cut off the ring finger, signet and all, to carry back as proof.
- **Thomas A. Invictus no longer exists as a name.** First Light became **Midnight** when it
  went into Veck; Shar took his first name as payment. He is **Justiciar Invictus**, remembers
  nothing else, and is untroubled by that — which is the part that should trouble the table.
- **Harrow Vane is dead**, bisected by Goldthorn. The temple's surviving staff — at least a
  zealot, an enforcer and an acolyte on the entrance floor — watched a Dark Justiciar walk out.
- **Greenrest's tree budded** — hundreds of buds, first blossom open. The town has never seen it
  bloom in living memory and is calling it a miracle. The four PCs know it is a count.
- **The party is level 6 before Monday** (called on the battle + third heart + Invictus's dark
  infusion).

## 2 · Now canon — said out loud, on tape

- **Six months, not eighteen.** The Widows' Ledger stops six months ago. Opened the session with
  the correction; everywhere.
- **Veck was the supplier.** Wend's ledger puts him through Greenrest every couple of months,
  Fen Trail out with sealed boxes, back without them, deposits landing within a day or two of
  each trip. Last seen ~six months ago heading back to Daggerford.
- **Veck's confession, verbatim beats:** started by *selling names* — widows, people with nobody
  left to ask after them; a careful scribe in the ledger office started pulling threads; he went
  to the house *to frighten him*; it went wrong; *"I know I killed your family."*
- **Shar's interest is stated, not implied.** Through Harrow: she does not care who holds the
  heart. She cares only that it *never becomes whole*, because the archfey is **a demigoddess,
  an old friend of both Chauntea and Selûne**, and Shar wants her in loss forever. *"As long as
  the daughter of her allies suffers, she is happy."*
- **The whole heart = four true resurrections.** Harrow offered that explicitly as the price of
  never joining it — Thomas's wife and children back, and *"even with Shar's magic, we can extend
  it."*
- **The dryad's second dream is an offer, not grief:** *stay, I will make it summer for you
  forever, be as I am, never end.* Jetten's Nature check confirmed the lore — dryads are fae
  punished into trees for loving mortals.
- **The Tideheart beats harder near the tree** and quieter walking away. Gren tested it.
- **The Duskheart:** advantage on Wisdom saves, and *Modify Memory*. Very Shar.
- **Invictus's oath stands.** Ruled at the table, mechanically neutral: *"plot things can happen,
  but it's not like you're going to lose your powers."* That is a promise to honour literally.

## 4 · Threads left open

1. **The green heart.** No lead. The remaining candidate from Sera's map is the dragon's wood.
   Morgash said *"we're leaving the dragon to the end"*; the party wants ranged weapons first.
2. **Greenrest reacts to a severed finger.** Four adventurers who left to *"talk to some
   Sharrans"* return with a merchant's finger and the news that he's dead. Oswin has just
   started trusting them. Wend liked Veck.
3. **Oswin's dream.** He'll have one. Decide whether it's the shared dream, something addressed
   to him as mayor, or nothing — and what he does with it.
4. **Selma's stronger potion.** If it works, the town goes back to sleep just as the dryad
   wakes. If she distributes it, the PCs might be the only ones dreaming again.
5. **The order of Shar knows.** A named enemy with a stated goal (keep the heart broken) and a
   face to hunt (a Dark Justiciar carrying Midnight). Harrow was a high priest; someone is above
   him.
6. **Who was Thomas?** Nobody has yet asked Invictus what his first name was, and he does not
   know. The party heard it happen. Morgash said *"you will regret this"* twice.
7. **Did the raven-haired man say yes?** Dream 3 writes itself.
8. **Daggerford.** Veck sold to the hags and bought from somewhere. The Daggerford end of the
   trade — the ledger office, whoever else knew — is untouched.
9. **The Iris.** Nobody opened the stone eye. Nobody went back up to the four hooded faces.
