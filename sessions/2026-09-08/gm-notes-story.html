1 · What changed tonight

  • Three of four hearts. Graveheart (Morgash), Tideheart (Gren, attuned overnight in Greenrest), Duskheart (Jetten, off Harrow's chest, attuned on the spot). The Sisters' Notes said GREEN, BLACK — not yet found; black is found. Green is the last piece and nobody has a location.
  • Renlow Veck is dead — executed in a cell, chained and unarmed, by Thomas, over Morgash's arm. Morgash then cut off the ring finger, signet and all, to carry home as proof.
  • Thomas A. Invictus no longer exists as a name. First Light became Midnight when it went into Veck; Shar took his first name as payment. He is Justiciar Invictus, remembers nothing else, and is untroubled by it — which is the part that should trouble the table.
  • Harrow Vane is dead, bisected by Goldthorn. At least a zealot, an enforcer and an acolyte on the entrance floor watched a Dark Justiciar walk out.
  • Greenrest's tree budded — hundreds of buds, first blossom open. The town calls it a miracle. The four PCs know it is a count.
  • Level 6 before Monday (called on the battle + third heart + Invictus's dark infusion).

2 · Now canon — said out loud, on tape

  • Six months, not eighteen. The Widows' Ledger stops six months ago. Corrected at the top of the session; everywhere.
  • Veck was the supplier. Wend's ledger puts him through Greenrest every couple of months, Fen Trail out with sealed boxes and back without them, deposits landing within a day or two of each trip. Last seen ~six months ago heading for Daggerford.
  • Veck's confession, beat by beat: started by selling names — widows, people with nobody left to ask; a careful scribe in the ledger office started pulling threads; he went to the house to frighten him; it went wrong; "I know I killed your family."
  • Shar's interest is stated, not implied. She does not care who holds the heart, only that it never becomes whole, because the archfey is a demigoddess, an old friend of both Chauntea and Selûne, and Shar wants her in loss forever. "As long as the daughter of her allies suffers, she is happy."
  • The whole heart = four true resurrections — offered explicitly as the price of never joining it: wife and children back, "and even with Shar's magic, we can extend it."
  • Dream 2 is an offer, not grief: stay, I will make it summer for you forever, be as I am, never end. Jetten's Nature check confirmed the lore — dryads are fae punished into trees for loving mortals.
  • The Tideheart beats harder near the tree and quieter walking away. Gren tested it.
  • The Duskheart: advantage on Wisdom saves, and Modify Memory.
  • Invictus's oath stands. Ruled at the table, mechanically neutral: "plot things can happen, but it's not like you're going to lose your powers." Honour that literally.

4 · Threads left open

  1. The green heart. No lead. The remaining candidate from Sera's map is the dragon's wood; Morgash said "we're leaving the dragon to the end" and wants a ranged weapon first.
  2. Greenrest reacts to a severed finger. Four adventurers return with a merchant's finger and the news that he is dead. Oswin has just started trusting them. Wend liked Veck.
  3. Oswin's dream. The shared dream, something addressed to him as mayor, or nothing — and what he does with it.
  4. Selma's stronger potion. If it works, the town goes back to sleep just as the dryad wakes. The PCs might be the only ones dreaming again.
  5. The order of Shar knows. A named enemy with a stated goal (keep the heart broken) and a face to hunt (a Dark Justiciar carrying Midnight). Harrow was a high priest; someone is above him.
  6. Who was Thomas? Nobody has asked Invictus what his first name was, and he does not know. The party heard it happen. Morgash said "you will regret this" twice.
  7. Did the raven-haired man say yes? Dream 3 writes itself.
  8. Daggerford. Veck sold to the hags and bought from somewhere. The ledger office, and whoever else knew, is untouched.
  9. The Iris. Nobody opened the stone eye. Nobody went back up to the four hooded faces.

5 · Loot with story weight

  • Midnight — Invictus. It announced itself. Treat it as a character.
  • The Duskheart — Jetten, attuned. Modify Memory on the ranger who was the party's conscience about Harrow's bargain.
  • Vesper Staff — Gren. Harrow's staff, never used against them.
  • Robe of Protection — Morgash, over the Periapt of Wound Closure.
  • A finger and a signet ring — Morgash's pack. The sheet item currently describes only the ring; the fiction is the finger (see mechanics).
  • The temple, stripped — the palm-worn gold disc (something rested a hand there for a very long time), the violet basin stone (glows faintly in a closed fist), gold tesserae off two defaced suns, a rack of the temple's dream-syrup, five holy symbols, star charts with no sun on them. Any of these is a hook if a buyer in Greenrest recognises it.
