2 · Automation that cost time — ranked by minutes lost

  1. Careful Spell + Fireball. Both Fireballs auto-rolled Dex saves for the excluded allies and auto-applied damage: four reverts the first time, five the second — and the second revert is what un-killed Gren, who went to 0 from his own spell through a timer-rolled save. Careful Spell needs to actually exclude the chosen creatures from the save prompt, or the prompt needs to stop auto-rolling.
  2. The 24-second decision timer auto-resolves saves. It fired on two Fireball saves, a Spirit Guardians save and a shield-bash offer. Auto-rolling a save on timeout is the dangerous case — it is how Gren dropped. Longer timer, or timeout = re-prompt rather than roll.
  3. Spirit Guardians rolled a save for Gren out of range. Flagged as a bug and reverted. The emanation is catching tokens outside the aura, or the wall isn't blocking.
  4. Stale Shield effect after a revert. The rewind left a Shield effect on Gren, so the reaction wasn't offered on the next hit and that damage was reverted by hand too. Reverts should clear effects they applied.
  5. Private rolls toggled on by accident at the start of the floor fight. Cosmetic; confused two turns.

3 · Rulings made — keep consistent

  • Divine Smite is melee-weapon only. No javelin smites.
  • Find Steed is largely flavour indoors. The steed doesn't come into the dungeon.
  • Dreadful Strike: once per turn, uses = Wisdom modifier.
  • Prayer of Healing: ten-minute cast, grants short-rest benefits. Used mid-dungeon; it changed the boss fight (52 HP across 8 hit dice plus superiority dice and sorcery points back). Expect it every session now.
  • Sorcerous Restoration costs five sorcery points for a 3rd-level slot. Gren used the Pearl of Power instead — which became Fireball #2.
  • Non-proficiency with a tool kit = no proficiency bonus, not inability to use it.
  • Bog Staff is not magical. 1d8 plus stale bullywug poison, no +2.
  • Careful Spell RAW is what was ruled: excluded allies take nothing. The automation is what's wrong, not the ruling.
  • Vow of Enmity was pointed out to Invictus and never used. It's free advantage for a minute — remind him.
