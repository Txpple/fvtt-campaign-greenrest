Justiciar Invictus opened on Harrow alone and hit twice out of two for **30** (24 slashing,

Seven foes: 3 Zealots, 2 Enforcers, 2 Acolytes. **523 damage dealt, 113 taken.**

| Combatant | Dealt | Accuracy | Taken | Saves |

| Invictus | 84 (21.0/rd), 6 hits | 5/6 (83%) | 33 | 2/3 |

**How it actually went.**

- **Round 1 was decided by one arrow.** An Acolyte opened with *Spirit Guardians* — a 15-ft
  necrotic aura that would have ticked on the party every round. Jetten's first longbow shot
  did 26 (longbow + *Hunter's Mark* + *Dreadful Strike*) and she rolled **5 against DC 13**.
  The aura was gone before it damaged anyone but Morgash, who had already failed his save.
- **Morgash then killed her outright** — a 32 to hit with the Maul of Momentum, 18 damage, and
  *Great Weapon Master — Hew* handed him a free follow-up.
- **Jetten was the target all fight.** He took 58 of the 113 the party lost, was tripped prone
  three times by Enforcers' *Trip Attack*, and **went to 0** to a Zealot's halberd in round 2.
  Invictus's *Lay on Hands* (10 points) brought him straight back up.
- **Hold Person paralysed Morgash** for a turn; it ended when Fireball #2 killed the Acolyte
  concentrating on it.
- **Fireball #2 also dropped Gren** — 17 through his own save — which the table rewound as a
  save-timer artifact. Not counted here.
- It closed with Invictus killing a Zealot, Jetten shooting the last one, and **Prayer of
  Healing** buying a free short rest with Harrow audible down the corridor.

**193 dealt to him, 88 from him.** A proper boss statblock, played properly.

**What Harrow did with the action economy.** Two legendary actions every round, every one of
them *Necrotic Burst*, and **every one aimed at Morgash** — 13, 13, 7 and a miss. That is why
Morgash took 75 of the 88 dealt in this fight while the other three took 13 between them. Add
*Unholy Word* (stunned Jetten at 8 vs 16, and broke Invictus's concentration on *Bane*), one
**Legendary Resistance** spent to shrug off a shield-bash knockdown, and a **Flame Strike** at
DC 18 that dropped Morgash to 0.

**What the party did back.** *Bane* landed (Harrow failed at 11 vs 13). Morgash crit with the
Pike, took the *Hew* attack, missed, and converted it with **Precision Attack — 15 + 2 = 17
against AC 17**. Invictus put a *Divine Smite* on top of a Midnight hit. Gren burned *Innate
Sorcery* for one advantaged Fire Bolt. And Jetten finished it: **Goldthorn at the waist, chest
and legs parting company.**

## 6 · What the buffs and features actually bought

### The winners

- **Careful Spell — 2 sorcery points, 188 damage prevented.** See §1. Nothing else this session
  is in the same order of magnitude.
- **Bless — eight flips, after exactly one last session.** The die actually earned its
  concentration this time:

  | Flip | Roll | Die | Result |
  | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
  | Jetten vs Acolyte (AC 16) | 16 | +3 | miss → hit |
  | Gren vs DC 15 Fireball | 15 | +4 | fail → save |
  | Invictus vs Zealot (AC 17) | 18 | +2 | miss → hit |
  | Gren, DC 10 concentration | 13 | +4 | kept *Bless* |
  | Jetten, DC 10 concentration | 12 | +3 | kept *Hunter's Mark* |
  | Jetten vs Harrow (AC 17) | 17 | +3 | miss → hit |
  | Morgash vs Harrow (AC 17) | 17 | +1 | miss → hit |
  | **Invictus vs DC 18 Flame Strike** | 18 | +2 | **fail → save** |

- **Shield Master, twice over.** The bash knocked Harrow prone in the opening round and forced
  him to spend a **Legendary Resistance** later. Then its reaction turned that saved Flame
  Strike from half damage into **none** — so the last row of the Bless table and the Shield
  Master reaction, stacked, took Invictus from ~19 damage to zero.
- **Relentless Endurance.** Flame Strike put Morgash at 0; he stood up at 1 and kept swinging.
  No death saves were rolled this session because of it.
- **Jetten's opening shot.** 26 damage that broke *Spirit Guardians* on the round it was cast —
  the highest-leverage single action of the floor fight.

### The duds, named as plainly as the winners

- **Topple went 2 for 7.** Last session the Maul of Momentum knocked things prone 86% of the
  time; tonight the Enforcer (20), two Zealots (19, 23) and Harrow twice (18, 23) all made the
  DC 15 Constitution save. Same weapon, tougher saves.
- **Innate Sorcery changed nothing measurable.** Spell save DC 15 → 16, and Gren forced no
  saves while it was up. It bought advantage on one Fire Bolt for 9 damage.
- **Bane lasted about a round.** It landed on Harrow, then Unholy Word broke Invictus's
  concentration on a 5 against DC 10. No flip is attributable to it.
- **Precision Attack split 1-for-2** — one die turned a miss into a hit on Harrow (13 damage),
  one was spent on a 12 + 5 = 17 against AC 19 and still missed.
- **Riposte 1-for-2** for Morgash — one whiff against an Enforcer, one 13-damage answer to
  Harrow. The Enforcer riposted him back and missed too.

### Healing

**Invictus carried it.** *Lay on Hands* spent its whole 25-point pool — 15 into Morgash, then
**10 to raise Jetten off the floor** — plus 31 more out of combat, *Prayer of Healing* for a
free short rest, and *Aid* for +5 max HP on three of them. The short rest itself returned
**52 HP across 8 hit dice** (Jetten 3, Invictus 3, Morgash 1, Gren 1) and is the only reason
the party met Harrow at anything like full strength.

The Sharrans healed too: a Zealot burned a bonus action on **Lay on Hands for 25** on himself,
which bought him about a round.

## 7 · Luck, and the shape of it

**This was a clean win, and the dice were not the reason.** The party out-damaged the
opposition 746 to 201 and never had two PCs down at once.

Where the dice did bite: **Morgash made 1 of 6 saving throws all session** — 0 for 4 on the
temple floor (including the Spirit Guardians save and the Hold Person that cost him a turn) and
1 for 2 against Harrow, the failure being the Flame Strike that dropped him. He is the party's
worst save profile and he is also the one the boss correctly focused; both fights found that.

The **Sharran Zealots rolled 3 natural 20s** between them and still only landed 6 of 12
attacks. Jetten took two natural 1s and was tripped prone three times, which is most of why he
absorbed 58 damage in the floor fight — the Enforcers' *Trip Attack* into advantage was a
genuinely good line of play against the party's least-armoured front-liner.

**Advantage economy:** Gren ×5, Jetten ×5, Morgash ×2, Invictus ×2, and the enemy ×5.
