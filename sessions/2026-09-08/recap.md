# Session 7 — The Temple of Shar

**2026-09-08 · 3h26m recorded · Gren, Jetten, Morgash, Thomas (→ Invictus)**

---

## TL;DR

The party sold the hag hoard in Greenrest, and the gilt-framed portrait they had kept out of
the sale turned out to be the one thing worth more than all of it: Mother Wend recognised the
sitter as **Renlow Veck**, the merchant who killed Thomas's wife and children. They rode north
to the Temple of Shar, where **Harrow Vane** was waiting with Veck chained in a cell and an
offer: take him, take the Duskheart, take a title. Thomas took all three. He killed Veck in
cold blood over Morgash's arm, First Light went black in his hand and named itself **Midnight**,
and Shar's price was his first name — he is **Justiciar Invictus** now and cannot remember what
he used to be called. Then he refused the actual terms, and they killed Harrow Vane and took
the heart anyway. Three of four.

---

## 1 · The correction, and the night before

The session opened with a retcon the DM called out directly: the Widows' Ledger stops **six
months ago**, not eighteen. Six months everywhere.

Back in Greenrest at nightfall the shops were shut, so the party took the inn. Gren claimed a
human-sized double bed to himself, refused to share it, and scattered caltrops in front of the
door in case of Pip.

**Dream 2 — "The Summer."** All four on the same hillside, high summer, the valley in bloom.
A blonde woman under the great tree and a raven-haired man walking up to meet her; the valley
blooms harder every time he smiles. Then the montage — seasons turning, never quite winter,
more silver in his hair each visit, and none in hers. At the end the two of them sit under the
tree, his silver head on her shoulder, neither smiling, both knowing. She says: *stay. I will
make it summer for you forever. Be as I am. Never end.*

Jetten's moonstone glowed green again as he went into trance.

Nature checks after: Jetten 18, Gren 8 (with advantage as a native of the Misty Forest). The
lore landed — dryads are fae who loved mortals and were punished into trees. The dreamer under
Greenrest's tree is the dryad, and this dream is her offer, not her grief.

Gren attuned to **the Tideheart** overnight (Death Ward 1/day, advantage on Constitution
saves), dropping his attunement to the Wand of Magic Missiles to make room.

## 2 · The morning — the tree buds

Ambrose met them in the common room: nobody was eating, everyone was outside. **The tree
budded overnight — hundreds of buds, and one blossom already open.** A town that has never in
living memory seen it bloom stood around it calling it a miracle.

The four of them know it is a count. One heart, two hearts, three.

Gren confirmed it by walking the Tideheart toward the tree — the heart beats stronger as it
gets closer, and quieter as it goes away.

## 3 · Breakfast with the mayor

Oswin Applewhite pulled them into a small restaurant off the square for eggs and asked it
plain: *for fifteen years I never once had to think about that tree. Now it's all anyone talks
about.* He credited them with Longshadow and the displacer beasts, and then asked the real
question — **what is it you keep bringing back to our home?**

It went badly first. Gren pushed on the dryad and on Chauntea having forsaken the town's
priest; Thomas opened the body-parts trade and got a flat denial (*not in this town, sir, not
without my knowledge*); Oswin listed the free room and board and the unpaid-for eggs and stood
up to leave, genuinely angry: *it baffles me that you don't trust us when we've done nothing
but give our open arms to you.*

**Morgash saved it.** He gave Oswin the honest, non-secret version — that beyond the town's
edge, where Greenrest doesn't go, there have been evil things, and all the party has been doing
is cleaning them out so the town can prosper; and that the tree started recovering as they did
it. Oswin took it, thanked him for finally being honest, and went off to his tower to process.
The hearts stayed unmentioned by unanimous vote (Jetten and Morgash both argued for holding
them back; Gren wanted to tell).

Thomas got one more thing out of him: a suggestion that Oswin skip his next dose of the tonic
and compare notes on the dreams. **Oswin agreed** — last dose four days ago, and he will not
take the next one.

## 4 · Selma, and Wend

**Selma the apothecary** doesn't like change. She confirmed the town has not dreamed in
generations — children start the potion at six — and that this morning was the first time in
her life anyone reported a dream. Her response is to try to make the potion *stronger*. She
gave Sera's Longbow back for free rather than sell it; **Morgash took it**.

**Mother Wend** is where the session turned. Shown the gilt-framed portrait, she recognised the
sitter instantly — the green waistcoat, and the gold signet ring he liked to show off. She
checked her ledgers: **Renlow Veck**. Came through every couple of months, always paid, took
the Fen Trail out with sealed boxes and came back without them. Last seen roughly **six months
ago**, heading back to Daggerford. Long overdue.

Gren read the brass plate aloud — the "gift, with gratitude, for my best customers" line, and
the joke about never biting the hand that feeds you — and Wend, who had assumed the boxes were
apples, was appalled. She cross-referenced her own dates against the Widows' Ledger cipher: the
deposits land within a day or two of each of his trips through Greenrest.

Morgash rolled a 20 on Intimidation and secured the arrangement: **if Veck comes back, Wend
delays him and sends word to Oswin and the constable.**

Thomas, out loud, at the table: *I know who was responsible for my family's assassination.*

## 5 · Shopping

The hag hoard went to Wend at half list — five bags of tea, the water-stained silk, the
whalebone combs, both oil paintings, all three ornate teacups at 250 gp each, the tapestry, the
tarnished silver service, the wired dragon bones and five peryton feathers. Party stash came
out at **almost exactly 1,500 gp**.

They deliberately **did not sell the merchant's portrait**.

Gren withdrew 300 gp and bought **six Potions of Healing** from Selma, distributed two to
Jetten and one each to Thomas and Morgash. Thomas cast Find Steed on the way out and traded up
to a celestial white horse. Nobody bought the plate mail; nobody bought thieves' tools.

## 6 · The road, and the Iris

A day's march, a camp, no dream, and the second morning brought them to it: a great ring of
pale stone in an old meadow, broad steps up from the south, four hooded faces watching inward,
and at the centre a disc of pale stone cut in curved petals meeting at a point — a shut eye.
No door, nobody to meet them, and stairs cutting down under the stone on the far side.

Gren cast Speak with Animals and interviewed a squirrel named **Pudgy**, who reported people in
purple robes coming and going, that they live down the stairs, and that they mostly come out at
night. Pudgy got a nut and left a hero-worshipper.

Morgash's Survival check (21): no footprints in stone, but no cobwebs either. Well travelled.

## 7 · The parley — Harrow Vane

He came up to meet them at the bottom of the stair and did not seem surprised. He opened with
Thomas's full name and his dead wife's:

> *Thomas Invictus of Daggerford. Anne's husband. You'll forgive me — we've followed you and
> your work for some time.*

The pitch, in pieces:
- Your power does not come from Lathander. You're an Oath of Vengeance paladin; it comes from
  the oath, and the oath is built out of what was taken from you.
- Shar is the Lady of Loss. You have lost, and you turned it into power. That is rare and it is
  valuable.
- *We're not asking you to be a supplicant. We're asking you to be a leader. We want you to be
  a Justiciar of Shar.*
- Yes, they have a piece of the heart — **the Duskheart** — and yes, it can be part of the
  bargain.
- And they have Renlow Veck. *He's for you, Thomas.*

## 8 · The cell

Bare-chested, wrists bolted to the rock, ragged trousers, and the gold signet ring still on his
right hand. He started apologising before anyone spoke.

Thomas cast Thaumaturgy for the booming voice and asked why. Veck's confession came out
unprompted and complete: it started with **selling names** — widows, people with nobody left to
come asking. It was only ever money. Then a scribe in the ledger office started pulling threads
and would not stop, and he went to that scribe's house **to frighten him**. It went wrong. He
said *I know I killed your family* out loud.

Harrow, from the doorway: *that's it, Thomas. Become vengeance. This is what you were meant to
do.*

**Morgash physically stopped him.** Athletics 27 against Thomas's 17, with Jetten helping — a
grapple that held for about ten seconds, and an argument that was better than the grapple:

> *You are going to allow this guy that you just met, that knew everything you have been going
> through and never came to help you a single time — now he wants something from you… He is no
> friend. He deserves to die, but not here, not now. I told you when I met you that I will be
> with you, and I will help you get justice.*

Jetten worked the other side: *there's more to this than meets the eye. Harrow Vane wants
something in return. And Thomas — this isn't going to bring your family back.*

Thomas's rage carried him through the hold; the **Gauntlets of Ogre Power** broke Morgash's
grip. The DM put the choice explicitly and mechanically neutral — nothing about this costs you
your paladin powers, plot things can happen, it's up to you — and Thomas took it.

*Out of honour, I have to kill this guy. End of story.*

He plunged First Light into Veck's chest. Morgash, twice: *you will regret this.*

## 9 · Midnight, and the price

The sword did not flare.

The warmth went out of it. The vision of the Lathander paladin he had when he first looted it
was simply gone — *as if the god himself slinks away from you.* The rose-gold ran to ink, the
blade drank the candlelight, went black, and came back **Midnight**, crackling with purple
necrotic light. It announced itself.

Then Shar took her payment. **His first name.** He reached for it in memory and there was
nothing there — and it didn't trouble him, because why would it. He is **Justiciar Invictus**.
Later in the night, asked about it: *I don't know what you're talking about. My name's always
been Justiciar.*

Harrow pulled down his robe to show the **Duskheart** on a chain against his chest, and named
the actual price:

> *Never join it. Never wake the sleeping archfey. The heart must never become whole.*

Four pieces joined is four true resurrections — his wife, his children, and Shar's magic could
stretch it further. All Shar wants in exchange is that the archfey — a demigoddess, an old
friend of both Chauntea and Selûne — stays in loss forever. *As long as the daughter of her
allies suffers, she is happy.*

**Thomas refused.** *This man deserved to die, but we still have a job to do, and I can't agree
to that.* Harrow: *then I can't give you the heart.*

## 10 · The fight, in three parts

**The break (1 round).** Justiciar Invictus opened with a shield bash that put Harrow prone
(failed his save at 11 vs 15) and two hits with Midnight for 30. Harrow, unimpressed —
*did you really think this would be that easy?* — cast **Word of Recall** and left: *I'll see
you downstairs.*

Before following, **Morgash cut the ring finger off Veck's corpse**, signet and all, to carry
back to Greenrest as proof.

**The bluff.** Upstairs a zealot, an enforcer and an acolyte stood up and did not know what to
do. Invictus, holding a black blade, told them to kneel and kiss his hand — Intimidation 17 —
and one of them bowed: *My Dark Justiciar. I am here to serve.* He could not talk them into
turning on Harrow (*that makes no sense, he's the high priest*), but they let the party walk
past.

**The temple floor (4 rounds).** Three zealots, two enforcers, two acolytes. An acolyte opened
with Spirit Guardians and Jetten broke her concentration on the first shot of the round;
Morgash crit the same acolyte dead with the Maul of Momentum. It got ugly in the middle — a
zealot's halberd put **Jetten at 0** and Invictus's Lay on Hands brought him back, an acolyte
paralysed Morgash with Hold Person, and Gren's second Fireball killed the acolyte and an
enforcer while briefly dropping *Gren* (rewound at the table as a save-timer artifact). Gren's
two Careful-Spelled Fireballs did essentially the whole fight. It closed with Invictus killing
a zealot and Jetten shooting the last one down, then **Prayer of Healing** for a free short
rest while Harrow taunted them from down the hall.

**Harrow Vane (3 rounds).** A real boss: two legendary actions a round of Necrotic Burst (all
of it aimed at Morgash), Unholy Word that stunned Jetten and broke Invictus's Bane, one
Legendary Resistance burned to shrug off a shield bash, and a Flame Strike that **dropped
Morgash to 0 — Relentless Endurance put him back up at 1.**

The party answered with everything: Bane landing on Harrow, Morgash critting with the Pike and
converting a miss to a hit with Precision Attack, Invictus's Divine Smite, Gren's Innate
Sorcery, and Jetten finishing him — **Goldthorn took him at the waist, and his chest fell away
from his legs.**

## 11 · Spoils, and out

Dropped by Harrow: **Robe of Protection** (Morgash took it and dropped the Periapt of Wound
Closure to attune), the **Vesper Staff** (Gren — +2 spell attack and +2 spell save DC), and
**the Duskheart**, which **Jetten** took and attuned — advantage on Wisdom saving throws, and
*Modify Memory*, which the DM noted is a very Shar-like gift.

The party then went through the temple room by room and stripped it: the gold disc off the
reading-room plinth, the violet stone out of the veiled woman's basin, gold tesserae chiselled
out of the broken sun mosaics, the alchemist's rack of cave-bloom draught, five jet-and-silver
holy symbols, a folio of star charts with no sun on them, and a bolt of sanctuary velvet.
**~1,500 gp of temple loot** into the stash.

Invictus bluffed the party out past the guards on the entrance floor before anyone worked out
what had happened downstairs. They made the road home and reached Greenrest at the town line,
where a scout waved them in — the fifth or sixth night they've come back to that town.

**Level 6 next session.** The DM called it on the battle, the third heart, and Invictus's dark
infusion of power.

## Where things stand

- **Three of four hearts:** Graveheart (Morgash), Tideheart (Gren, attuned), Duskheart (Jetten,
  attuned). The remaining piece is the **green** one, still unfound.
- **Thomas is gone as a name.** Justiciar Invictus carries Midnight, has no first name, and
  told Harrow to his face that they *will* be combining the four pieces.
- **Renlow Veck is dead**, killed in a cell, unarmed and chained — and Greenrest doesn't know
  yet. Morgash has the finger and the ring.
- **Harrow Vane is dead.** The temple's remaining staff last saw a Dark Justiciar of Shar walk
  out of the front door.
- **Oswin is off the potion** as of this morning, by agreement, and Selma is trying to brew a
  stronger one.
- Shar has stated her actual interest plainly: she does not care who holds the heart, only that
  it never becomes whole.
