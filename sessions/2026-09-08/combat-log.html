The Broken Heart of Greenrest — Session 7

Combat Log — The Temple of Shar

8 September 2026 · 3 combats · folded from 177 Battle Flow stamped messages of 619

Three fights, all underground, all in the same building: a one-round break-off where the high priest declined to fight and teleported away, a four-round grind against seven Sharrans on the temple floor, and a three-round boss with legendary actions. The party dealt 746 and took 201. One PC went down and was raised; one hit zero and stood straight back up. Nobody rolled a death save all night.

Two sorcery points prevented 188 damage. Gren's two Fireballs both caught the entire party in the radius. Rolled out of the actual saves, they would have taken 66 from the first and 122 from the second — 188 total, against the 201 the party actually lost across the whole session. Careful Spell is the reason the floor fight was a win rather than a rout, and it is the second session running that it has topped this table.

Jetten took 58 of the party's 113 here, and that was tactics rather than luck. The Enforcers used Trip Attack on him three times, he failed all three Strength saves (13, 11, and one more against DC 15), and every follow-up came at advantage against a prone target. He is the least-armoured member of the front line and the enemy found that within a round. He went to 0 to a Zealot's halberd and Lay on Hands put him straight back up.

Morgash failed every save he rolled in this fight — 0 for 4. Spirit Guardians, a Fireball, and the Hold Person that cost him a full turn paralysed. Nothing about the encounter was overtuned; his save profile is simply the party's softest and two different Sharrans exploited it correctly.

4 · Harrow Vane — 03 Temple of Shar, 3 rounds

193 dealt to him, 88 from him. He used the boss kit properly: two legendary actions every round, every one of them Necrotic Burst, and every one aimed at Morgash — 13, 13, 7 and one miss. Add Unholy Word (stunned Jetten at 8 vs DC 16 and broke Invictus's concentration on Bane), one Legendary Resistance spent to refuse a shield-bash knockdown, and a Flame Strike at DC 18 that put Morgash at 0.

5 · What the buffs and features actually bought

Every spell, maneuver and mastery that touched a number this session, and what it produced. Ranked by how much it moved the fight.

The big three

  • Careful Spell — the best two sorcery points of the campaign so far. Fireball #1 would have hit Morgash for 27 (failed) and Gren, Jetten and Invictus for 13 each (saved): 66. Fireball #2 would have hit Jetten, Invictus and Morgash for 35 each (all failed) and Gren for 17 (saved): 122. 188 damage prevented, while both blasts landed at full value on seven Sharrans for roughly 246.
  • Bless finally earned its concentration — eight flips. Last session it flipped exactly one outcome all night. This session: three misses turned into hits (Jetten vs an Acolyte at AC 16, Invictus vs a Zealot at AC 17, Jetten and Morgash both vs Harrow at AC 17), two concentration saves held that would have dropped (Gren's Bless, Jetten's Hunter's Mark), one Fireball save flipped for Gren — and, decisively, Invictus's Flame Strike save at DC 18, made on an 18 only because of a +2 die.
  • Shield Master, stacked on top of that same save. Its reaction turns a successful Dex save against an area effect into no damage at all, so Bless plus Shield Master took Invictus from roughly 19 damage to zero on one Flame Strike. The bash also put Harrow prone in round one of the break and forced a Legendary Resistance later.

Healing that mattered

Invictus carried the party's staying power. Lay on Hands emptied its whole 25-point pool — 15 into Morgash, then 10 to raise Jetten off the floor — and he added 31 more out of combat, an Aid, and the Prayer of Healing that bought the free short rest. That rest returned 52 hit points across 8 hit dice (Jetten 3, Invictus 3, Morgash 1, Gren 1). Jetten drank a potion for 6 and healing-worded himself for 10; Morgash used Second Wind for 10 after the fight. The Sharrans healed too — a Zealot spent a bonus action on his own Lay on Hands for 25, which bought him roughly one extra round.
