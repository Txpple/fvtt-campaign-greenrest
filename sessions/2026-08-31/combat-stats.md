> **This was terrible luck, not a broken encounter.** Three of the four were under *Bless* and

> off.** All four failing at once lands a little over one percent — **on the order of a
> one-in-ninety night.** Gren then spent Heroic Inspiration on the reroll and came up 9 against
> a 13. The sphere even killed one of the caster's own warriors. On any ordinary set of rolls
> two of them save, nobody drops, and this reads as a hard opening fight rather than a near-wipe.

## 6 · What the buffs and features actually bought

### The big three

- **Careful Spell — the single best resource spend of the night.** For one sorcery point, Gren's
  Fireball excluded all four allies from a blast that would otherwise have caught every one of
  them. Thomas *failed* his save outright and would have taken the full 29; Gren, Jetten and
  Morgash all saved and would have taken 14 apiece. **One sorcery point prevented 71 damage** —
  a third of everything the party lost all session — while still landing full damage on four
  enemies.
- **Riposte turned two enemy misses into 36 damage.** Two superiority dice on reaction attacks,
  both hits: 16 in the swamp, 20 in the treetop (which also toppled the target).
- **Hunter's Mark carried 30 force damage** across four fights — about a sixth of Jetten's total
  output — and he kept moving it with a bonus action rather than recasting, so it cost one slot
  all night. *Dread Ambusher* added another 23 psychic.

| **Bless** (Fey-Touched) | free cast | **Exactly one flip all night** — a d4 of 2 turning a miss into a hit. It also added its die to three of the four Vitriolic Sphere saves, and all three still failed; the concentration broke to that same spell |

### Healing that mattered

Three raises from zero, all in the swamp: **Jetten's *Healing Word*** on Thomas, **Gren's
*Healing Word*** on Thomas again after the lingering acid dropped him a second time, and
**Morgash's Potion of Healing** on Jetten. Thomas's *Lay on Hands* put 10 into Gren before any of
it. The *Rope Trick* short rest was the real recovery, and Thomas's **Healer** feat quietly
doubled it at **+3 HP per hit die** party-wide — the only reason the second half of the session
was comfortable.
