# Session 6 — "The Widow Fen"

_2026-08-31 · 3h 08m · Gren Greenmantle (Tom) · Jetten Elisedil (Andrew) ·
Morgash the Gravemaker (Anthony) · Thomas A. Invictus (Robert)_

**TL;DR.** The party marched northeast from the Temple Ruins into a drowning bog, and the very
first encounter nearly ended the campaign: a Bullywug Bog Sage dropped *Vitriolic Sphere* on all
four of them and put Thomas and Jetten on the floor. They clawed back, took a short rest inside
Jetten's *Rope Trick*, and cleared the treetop village room by room — finding the sisters' whole
grief-trade laid out in ledgers, jars, and charcoal sketches of Lae'zel. Downstairs, a crying
child named "Masie" turned out to be an illusion worn by a hag. The coven offered a partnership:
help gather the Heart, one beat for their dead sister Ethel, the other three for the party.
Thomas wanted the deal — the hags claimed to have the name of his family's killers. Jetten
refused. Hazel lost patience first. Both hags died in two rounds, and the Tideheart rolled out
of Hazel's hand as she fell. The party walked out with the blue shard and reached the edge of
Greenrest just before sundown.

---

## 1 · The road out, and the level-5 shakedown

The session opened on housekeeping at the Temple Ruins. Everyone took a long rest, and the table
spent twenty minutes on level-5 mechanics — Gren walked the group through upcasting (Bless at 2nd
level to cover all four), Thomas got the full 2nd-level paladin list added to his sheet mid-scene
and asked how prepared spells work, and Morgash attuned to the Graveheart and the Periapt of
Wound Closure, filling all three of his attunement slots alongside the Maul of Momentum.

Thomas cast **Faithful Steed** and got a fey horse — "part plant, part horse looking" — and rode
it out. The march northeast ran several hours; the ground softened, the muck-smell rose, and the
forest gave up at the edge of open black water. Out in the fog stood the great tree with cabins
and rope bridges in its crown, lights burning.

The opposed perception check went to the bullywugs. Nobody beat them outright, but Morgash rolled
a **natural 20 on Survival** and read the enemy: bullywugs, fey-born half-frog, normally
keeping to their own society — but these had a purplish-red glow behind the eyes that was not
natural. Enthralled. He relayed it to the party, and Jetten nocked an arrow.

## 2 · The swamp — the fight that almost ended it

**One spell decided this fight.** After an opening exchange — Jetten's *Hunter's Mark* and
*Dread Ambusher* opening strong, Morgash *Riposte*-ing a warrior who whiffed on him — the Bog
Sage leapt into range and cast **Vitriolic Sphere**: a 4th-level, 20-foot sphere of acid,
10d4 up front and 5d4 again at the end of its next turn.

**All four PCs failed the DC 13 Dexterity save.** Gren burned Heroic Inspiration on the reroll
and still failed. 28 damage landed on everyone at once; Jetten lost concentration on Hunter's
Mark, Gren lost Bless. The spell even killed one of the sage's own warriors — the DM had it
hop up and down, delighted with itself. Then the lingering acid ticked for 11 more and dropped
**both Thomas and Jetten** to zero.

The recovery was scrappy and cooperative:

- **Thomas** went down after a warrior's rapier caught him twice; his fey steed vanished with him.
- **Jetten** brought Thomas back with *Healing Word*, then killed a warrior with Goldthorn.
- **Gren** spent wand charges on *Magic Missile* — the magic missiles killed the Bog Sage —
  then healed Thomas up off the floor with his Magic Initiate *Healing Word*.
- **Morgash** carried the melee: *Precision Attack* twice to convert misses into hits, *Great
  Weapon Master*'s Hew for free follow-ups, and repeated **Topple** knockdowns with the Maul of
  Momentum. He also spent Second Wind and handed Jetten a Potion of Healing.
- **Thomas** finished the last warrior with a **Divine Smite**.

Final tally: the Bog Sage dealt **198 damage**; the four warriors between them managed 34.

**Rope Trick.** Jetten pulled the best trick of the night — casting *Rope Trick* and taking the
whole party into the extra-dimensional space for a short rest. Thomas's **Healer** feat turned
that rest into a windfall: +3 hit points per hit die spent, party-wide. Everyone came down at or
near full.

Gren looted the **Bog Staff** off the sage — 1d8+2 bludgeoning plus 3d6 poison — immediately
noticed it was better suited to a melee character, offered it around, and then kept it anyway.

## 3 · The treetop village — five quick fights and the whole story

Jetten scouted under **Pass without Trace** (+10 to everyone's Stealth), found a lone warrior on
the big central platform, and reported back. The party debated burning the huts down or cutting
the bridges before deciding to climb the tree nobody was watching. Thomas cast **Aid** on
Morgash, Jetten and himself — the first use of a spell added to his sheet that same evening.

Morgash's Stealth in splint armor came up a 2. They were spotted, and the croaking went up.

What followed was five separate one-round fights, all trivial: two bullywug warriors on the
bridges, then animated furniture in each room — brooms, a flying sword, and two Rugs of
Smothering. The party took **six points of damage across all five combats**. Gren, who had
whiffed three fights running, landed a *Sorcerous Burst* critical that snapped the flying sword
in half. Morgash's Hew triggered twice. Thomas's Shield Bash knocked the sword prone.

The rooms are where the session's actual weight sat:

- **The Widows' Ledger** — Thomas recognized the cipher on sight; it's the same one he copied a
  hundred times in Daggerford. An Investigation roll of **25** confirmed it: a bill-of-lading
  book for the delivery of body parts, with a drop-stone marked on the moor path they had walked
  past that afternoon. The entries stop. Thomas put it at a year and a half at the table — ⚠ RETCONNED 2026-09-06 to **six months**, matching his own backstory and the hags' "stopped bringing it months ago" — right around when
  his family died. He said it out loud: *"This is exactly why I'm on this quest."*
- **The Sisters' Notes** — read aloud by Jetten, and it reframed the night: *"The heart is
  broken in four. RED — the barrow. BLUE — ours, below. GREEN, BLACK — not yet found. The dryad
  sleeps in the great tree on Greenrest's square. The heart is hers. Whole, it beats four times.
  One beat brings back our sister... Propose partnership. Do not fight."* The party immediately
  worked out that "ours, below" meant a lower level, and that the hags knew they were coming.
- **The grief-jars** — Mourner's Teeth, Widow's Tears, and A Mother's Little Finger. Thomas
  stared at the last one: *"How's that not my wife's finger in the jar?"* Gren checked the teeth
  and told him, flatly and kindly, that they were adult teeth — nothing of his children's.
- **The Sheaf of Charcoal Sketches** — dozens of pages of the same hard-jawed, sharp-eyed woman
  with a greatsword. Morgash recognized **Lae'zel** instantly. On the back: *"HER. The one who
  took our sister. Do not let us forget her face."* The DM tied it off: Lae'zel had once told
  Morgash about killing a hag at a riverside teahouse outside Baldur's Gate.
- **The Riverside Teahouse Keepsake** — the pressed flower and the little carved sign,
  "ALL WELCOME," wrapped and rewrapped in oilcloth until the folds went soft. The easter egg
  landed; the table connected it to the sketches without being told.

Morgash's reaction to learning the hags had been drawing his ex-lover's face for years was to
say *"He lives here. I'm going to kill him,"* and then, a beat later, *"That woman was awesome."*

## 4 · The parlor — Masie, and the offer

Down the hollow trunk, past the waterline: candlelight where there should be none, the smell of
tea and peat, a wall of jars floor to ceiling, cages, and a table set for three with the tea
still steaming. And a little girl in a cage, finger to her lips.

Jetten picked the padlock with his dagger. "Masie" thanked them, said she'd been taken, and
started asking questions: how many of you are there? They said you were bringing a stone — do
you have the stone? What are your names?

**Gren rolled Insight 18** and flagged her as wrong. It was Morgash who broke it — the "girl"
walked up and started *smelling* him, said **"I smell Lae'zel on you,"** and he swung at her.
His hand passed straight through. The illusion dropped, and two hags stepped out of the dark.

The offer was made plainly: *"Not only did she kill our sister, but she killed your heart too."*
Then the pitch — help us gather the Heart, one resurrection for our sister Ethel, the rest are
yours. *"Resurrect the world for all we care. We just need our sister."*

**Thomas wanted the deal**, and for a specific reason: the hags said they had the name of the
people who supplied them from Daggerford. *"I don't care about your family. But I know who did
it — the same ones who bring us the body parts."* They wouldn't hand it over without a bargain,
and the bargain they wanted was an **unbreakable bond**: *"If either of us fails, you die."*
Thomas offered a paladin's oath instead. Hazel: *"The oath of a vengeance paladin means nothing
to me."*

Jetten refused outright. Gren pushed for information and got nothing free. Hazel called it:
**"These aren't going to bargain with us. Let's kill them, sister, and be done with this."**

## 5 · The cavern fight — two rounds

It was not close. Both hags were at 86 hit points and the party opened at full strength.

- **Hazel** landed *Suggestion* on Gren, who saved with advantage, then *Hex*.
- **Gren** answered with **Innate Sorcery** and a **Careful Spell Fireball** — every ally
  excluded — for 29, wiping the animated armor, the broom, and the flying sword in one cast.
  Spellfire Burst finished the armor's last four hit points. He got a line in too: *"I look at
  Hazel and say — I did not forget any of my spells."*
- **Morgash** critically hit Mabel with the Maul for 19, Action Surged, toppled her twice, and
  she had to **Misty Step** out. He told her: *"No matter where you go, I'm going to find you."*
- **Thomas** broke Hazel's concentration on Hex with First Light plus a **Divine Smite**.
- **Jetten** killed **Mabel** with a longbow shot, then moved Hunter's Mark to Hazel.
- Hazel, bloodied and prone, refused the offer of surrender: *"You just killed my sister — do
  you think I'm going to make any deal with you? I'd rather die."*

Gren tried *Hold Person* to take her alive for interrogation. She saved. Morgash asked whether
he could subdue rather than kill; Hazel looked up at him, spat, and said **"Do it. I have
nothing to live for."** He smashed her skull.

A blue amulet rolled out of her hand as she died — **the Tideheart**. Whoever picked it up got
the same vision the Graveheart had shown: the dryad weeping, her heart bursting, the pieces
scattering, the mists driven back from a green valley.

## 6 · After the fight

Thomas went straight to the jar-wall. Sources, prices, dates — years of them, all in the same
hand as the ledger upstairs. Every Daggerford shipment accounted for. **These two hags were the
buyers.** And nowhere on that wall is the name of the procurer.

The rest of the room: a huge crocodile skeleton used as a kitchen rack; cages holding a kettle,
a fiddle, and bellows, all gone inert; and one cage with packed straw and **a child's skeleton
with the name "Masie" scratched on the chest.** The real one died a long time ago.

They looted the tea table — three ornate teacups, bags of rare tea, paintings, silk, dragon
bones, a tapestry, a gilt-framed portrait of a merchant nobody recognized (Jetten: *"it might be
somebody that somebody in town might recognize"*). Roughly 3,100 gp of sellables.

Then out through a marsh-plant-screened corridor to the surface, and back to Greenrest, arriving
at the edge of town just before sundown. A scout waved them in. **Session ended at the town
line** — the homecoming and Dream A.2 are next session.

## 7 · Where things stand

- **Two shards of four:** the Graveheart (red, Morgash, attuned) and the **Tideheart** (blue,
  party stash, unattuned). Green and black are unfound.
- **The Greenrest dryad is confirmed**, by the hags themselves: she broke her own heart, she is
  trapped under the great tree in the town square through her own grief, and she is older than
  they are. The party knows this now.
- **The partnership is dead** — both hags are dead and Ethel stays dead.
- **Thomas's thread stalled hard.** He has the ledger, the cipher, the drop-stone, and proof the
  ring's reach was real — and still no name. The hags claimed to have it and died with it.
- **The Morgash / Lae'zel bargain never got offered.** The hags went to violence before the
  heartbreak-in-a-bottle pitch. That offer is unspent.
- **The gilt-framed portrait** is an unintended live hook — the players decided on their own it
  might be someone Greenrest recognizes.
