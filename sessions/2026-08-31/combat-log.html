2 · The swamp — 01 Swamp, 3 rounds

The Bog Sage cast Vitriolic Sphere (4th level, 10d4 acid on impact plus 5d4 at the end of its next turn, 20-foot radius) onto the clustered party. All four failed the DC 13 Dexterity save; Gren burned Heroic Inspiration on the reroll and failed again. The lingering acid ticked a second time and dropped Thomas and Jetten.

Then Rope Trick for a safe short rest, with Thomas's Healer feat adding +3 per hit die spent, party-wide.

This was terrible luck, not a broken encounter. Three of the four were under Bless at the time, and the save was only DC 13. Working the odds back from the sheets: Morgash had a coin-flip, Thomas a shade better than one, Gren — the only one without the Bless die, having cast it on everyone but himself — was at 55%. And Jetten, with a +7 Dexterity save and a Bless die on top, had roughly a seven-in-eight chance of shrugging it off.

All four failing at once lands at a little over one percent — on the order of a one-in-ninety night. Then Gren spent Heroic Inspiration on the reroll and came up 9 against a 13. The sphere even killed one of the caster's own warriors. On any ordinary set of rolls two of them shrug it off, nobody goes down, and this reads as a hard opening fight rather than a near-wipe.

5 · What the buffs and features actually bought

Every spell, maneuver and mastery that touched a number this session, and what it produced. Ranked by how much it moved the fight.

The big three

  • Careful Spell was the single best resource spend of the night. For one sorcery point, Gren's Fireball excluded all four allies from a blast that would otherwise have caught every one of them. Thomas failed his save outright and would have eaten the full 29; Gren, Jetten and Morgash all saved and would have taken 14 apiece. One sorcery point prevented 71 damage — a third of everything the party lost all session — while still landing full damage on four enemies.
  • Riposte turned two enemy misses into 36 damage. Morgash spent two superiority dice on reaction attacks; both hit, one for 16 in the swamp and one for 20 in the treetop (which also knocked the target prone). That is a full extra turn's output taken on somebody else's initiative.
  • Hunter's Mark carried 30 force damage across four separate fights — about one sixth of Jetten's total output — and he kept moving it to new targets with a bonus action rather than recasting, so it cost him exactly one spell slot all night. Dread Ambusher added another 23 psychic on top.

Healing that mattered

Three separate raises from zero hit points, all in the swamp: Jetten's Healing Word put Thomas back on his feet, Gren's Healing Word did it again after the lingering acid dropped him a second time, and Morgash's Potion of Healing brought Jetten back. Thomas's Lay on Hands put 10 into Gren before any of that.

The Rope Trick short rest was the real recovery, though — and Thomas's Healer feat quietly doubled it, adding +3 hit points per hit die to every roll the party made. Between them the party spent most of its hit dice and walked into the treetop at or near full, which is the only reason the second half of the session was comfortable.
