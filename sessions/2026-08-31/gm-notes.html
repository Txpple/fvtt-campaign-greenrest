The parley was much shorter than planned. The quote bank barely got used: no Ethel name-drop in Mabel's voice, no Lae'zel mimicry beat, no shard-tax recitation, no "a heart has FOUR" line from a hag, and the Morgash bargain (cut the heartbreak out, bottled, taken in payment) was never offered. The scene went delight → the Lae'zel jab → the partnership pitch → the unbreakable-bond demand → Hazel's patience running out. Worth knowing that the pitch itself landed; it was the terms that broke it. Thomas was ready to sign.

Balance — the swamp needs a look before you reuse it

The Bog Sage out-damaged everything else in the session by a wide margin. It dealt 198 of the 219 total damage the party took across the whole night. A CR-4 caster with a 4th-level 20-ft-radius AoE against a clustered level-5 party at the first encounter of the night is a coin-flip on a TPK, and the DM said so at the table: "I don't understand why they gave bog sages that spell." Options if the statblock comes back: drop the slot level, give it a smaller radius, or don't open the session with it.

Contrast: the actual boss fight was trivial — the hags at 86 HP died in 2 rounds having dealt 23 damage between them. The difficulty curve ran exactly backwards.

Loose threads and hooks

  • THE NAME. Thomas's whole arc now hangs on a name the hags claimed to have and died holding. The jar-wall explicitly has no procurer name. Decide where the name actually lives — a second ledger in the fen they never searched? Someone in Daggerford? A Greenrest connection? The players will come looking, and Thomas said out loud that this is why he's on the quest.
  • The gilt-framed portrait of a stern merchant (renamed "…of a Smiling Merchant" 2026-09-06 with new DM art + description; the party snapshot keeps the as-played name) — pure flavor loot, but Jetten decided unprompted that it might be someone Greenrest would recognize, and Thomas asked "Not Applewhite, is it?" Free gift: make it someone. It costs nothing and they've already invested.
  • The dryad is now public knowledge to the party — trapped under the Greenrest tree, through her own grief, older than the hags, and the Heart is hers. They walked into town knowing this. The tree-budding beat lands differently now: they will read it as a count, exactly as intended.
  • The Morgash bargain is unspent — no hag ever offered to bottle his heartbreak. It can move to another hag, another fey, or come back through Ethel's memory. Don't lose it.
  • "Ours" — the empty jar was never found (the party missed that room's item or it never came up). Still available.
  • Volo's Guide (Annotated) was looted but never read aloud at the table — the three-hands easter egg didn't land. Worth a nudge if anyone opens it.
  • Lae'zel is now firmly on the board for Anthony: the sketches, the teahouse keepsake, and the DM's confirmation that she killed a hag at a riverside teahouse near Baldur's Gate. Morgash played it wistful rather than wounded ("that woman was awesome"), which is a different read than the plan assumed. Follow his lead.
