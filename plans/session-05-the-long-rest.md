# Session 5 — "The Long Rest"

_The Broken Heart of Greenrest · Tuesday 2026-08-25, 8 PM ET · Status: played 2026-08-25_

The whole session is Greenrest: recovery, investigation, roleplay, and the choice of shard site 2 —
with the Hollowing as the thing that finally drags them into the town they've been avoiding. One
fight, at the very end, as the flex item.

**Teaching session:** two mechanics get taught by play tonight — the **Exhaustion**
condition (Thomas's arc, §1–3) and the **Ready action** against flyers (§7).

## 1 · Open — the walk out, and Thomas failing

- Montage the exit: the tunnels are silent — the sobbing has stopped. The Hollow does not restock;
  the silence is proof they did something real. No encounters on the way up.
- First camp (the Kestrel camp, per their stated intent): dreamless — the first quiet night since
  they entered the valley's orbit. One line, no explanation.
- Thomas opens the session at **Exhaustion 1** — the Hollowing took −20 of his 36; the deepest wound
  in the party by far. He can't fasten his own pack.
- The march makes him worse. By the last miles he's at **Exhaustion 2** (−4 on d20 tests, −10 ft
  speed) and cannot walk unaided. A DC 10 Survival check in the woods finds a straight branch to cut
  into a rough cane — the same cane the whole town will watch him lean on. Between the cane and
  shoulders they get him home. Field rest is visibly doing nothing — the party watches the ruling
  before anyone states it.

## 2 · Arrival at dusk — a town buzzing about two things

They limp into a square running on two wonders that broke the same week:

- **The tree greened** — first new leaf in living memory. Wonder in the square; the elders quiet
  underneath it in a way the party may not notice.
- **The road is open** — one of Warin's scouts came back from the ruined camp with good news for the
  first time in years: the pack's carcasses, counted and cold. The town has done the arithmetic it's
  been afraid of for a generation: the pack was why travelers never came back. Maybe now they come.
  Trade. New faces. Greenrest spoken of in Daggerford as a place on a road, not a rumor behind the
  mist.

The braid: the town's two oldest constants — *the tree is sick* and *nobody comes here* — both broke
the same week, and both trace to the same four people. At one shard nobody's suspicious; it just
makes the welcome enormous. They get credit for two miracles and understand only one.

Then the buzz cuts off, because the crowd sees Thomas. One look and the town moves: the church has
the beds — someone run for Selma.

## 3 · The doctor — church and Selma in one stroke

- The infirmary wing is the town's sickroom; **Enid** receives them, kind and completely out of her
  depth. The party walks into the clue-in-stone building for the first time because they have to —
  tympanum on the way in, too-grand nave, fey trappings everywhere — investigation seeded for free,
  never forced. *(Optional garnish: Thomas's own Lay on Hands glows in a church where no prayer has
  ever been answered. Enid watches. Her first hairline crack.)*
- **Selma** arrives with her bag — the visit they've dodged for three sessions, delivered to them.
  Her verdict over the sickbed: the wound under the wound doesn't knit on the road. Several days of
  real rest, at least one full day and night of it in a bed — no marching, no fighting. Gren's
  Religion check heard it right but incomplete: the bed is the finisher, not the whole cure.
- **The cane:** Thomas can be up and about between bed stints — gray, slow, on his road-cut cane.
  The town watches the cost of its two miracles shuffle to the apothecary with children trailing
  him. Feeds the gratitude and the elders' quiet.

## 4 · Night one — Dream A.1, in town, mid-session

- The dream fires the first night they sleep in Greenrest **in real beds** — homecoming is the
  trigger; the road nights outside were dreamless, and the contrast teaches: the dreams belong to
  the place. Keep day one lean (arrival → church → triage → turn in) so A.1 lands mid-session while
  the table is fresh.

> **READ-ALOUD — Dream A.1.** Fires the first night in real beds; all four at once. Read it straight
> through:
>
> You are all four standing on the same hillside, and you know it — you can see one another, solid
> as daylight. The valley below is Greenrest's valley — the river, the ring of hills — but fuller:
> high summer laid on thick. Wheat gone gold, orchards heavy enough to prop, swallows cutting the
> warm light. It is the kind of afternoon a childhood keeps. At the crown of the hill stands one
> enormous tree in full green leaf, and under the tree a woman is waiting.
>
> She is young and blond and barefoot, and she is looking straight at you — through you — the way
> you look at a door you have watched for years: as if someone she loves is standing exactly where
> you stand. Her face opens with a joy so large it is hard to look at.
>
> "Corin? …There you are. I knew you'd come back — everything comes back to me. The green comes
> back, the swallows come back. Oh, love, you took so long. Stay this time — stay, and I'll make it
> summer for you forever."
>
> She holds out her hand. And each of you feels a weight in your own: a warm red stone, beating
> softly — held in fingers that are not yours. Cold fingers, closed tight, that will not open.
>
> The gold of the hillside tips, just at the edges — the light going gray, like a cloud you cannot
> see crossing the sun — and you wake, in beds, in Greenrest, all four at once. It is morning.
> Outside the window, the old gray tree is carrying its little new leaves like nothing in the world
> is wrong.

- It's **shared** — and Thomas sleeps in the infirmary while the others are at The Long Rest, so
  four people under two different roofs stand in one dream. Even stronger proof it's a place
  reaching, not a nightmare.
- **Morning debrief** over Ambrose's breakfast — with the greening tree visible out the window.
  Vision → dream → tree connects with the thing in view. Let them build it.
- Pocket rule: anyone who drinks tonic is shielded out of the shared dream. Their instinct has
  always been to dream on purpose; expect no problem.

## 7 · The road out — Temple Ruins, and the thing in the air

- **Site:** *Temple Ruins* — the imported ruin clearing, with fog VFX and the forest
  soundscape. In that soundscape, **Hawk Cry every 180 ± 60s IS Longshadow**, audible long before anyone
  looks up, alongside Crow Caws (which the Speak with Animals beat needs), Songbirds, and the Forest
  Day bed. An old ruin just off whichever road they chose; **nav stays off until the night**.
- **The open — a shadow on the grass.** As they cross the clearing, a man-shaped shadow slides over
  the ground and stops — hovering, cast by something circling above. **Perception DC 12:** something
  big is in the air, and it is the wrong shape for the shadow it casts. On a miss, Longshadow's
  first dive comes with surprise (his alone; the other follows next round). **Prop:** the pre-cut
  `longshadow-shadow.png` tile at 40% opacity, placed and hidden on the scene; reveal it, then
  drag it under him as he circles.
- **Gren + Speak with Animals:** the birds in that soundscape know the hunter. Cast at the crows:
  they call it "Longshadow," it hunts this stretch regularly, and it is not alone. The boss name
  lands diegetically and the ambush count leaks — a spell slot well spent.
- **The fight:** **Longshadow** (AC 15, 53 HP, STR 18) **+ 1 × Peryton** (CR 2, AC 13,
  33 HP). They cannot kite — perytons have no ranged
  attack; one that stays in the air is out of the fight. Rhythm: one or two strafing dives across
  the open clearing (**Dive bonus damage is DM-narrated** — it is not on Longshadow's sheet, which
  carries only Flyby, Talons, Gore, Multiattack; Flyby = no opportunity attacks on the way out) —
  then on first blood the peryton lands on that prey and commits: Gore + Talons every round, no
  retreat until it or the meal is finished. The tree line denies dive lanes — hugging it forces them
  in low and slow. Jetten's brand-new arrows earn their keep.
- **TEACHING BEAT — the Ready action.** The melee martials learn to ready attacks against swooping
  attackers ("when it comes at me, I swing"). Teach the tradeoff honestly: a readied attack is one
  attack (your reaction), **not** Extra Attack — readying vs repositioning is a real choice. Thrown
  javelins are the companion lesson. Let round 1 flail at the sky; prompt or let Jetten model it;
  round 2 the readied greatsword connects mid-swoop.
- **Budget honesty.** The party is **level 4**; level is decoupled from shards. Longshadow + 2
  perytons is ≈1,550–1,700 XP, which crowds the high end for four at level 4, so the default is to
  **drop the second peryton** (≈1,150 XP, a solid moderate fight).
  *The honest counter-argument, if you'd rather keep both:* this fight happens **after** three days
  of town rest — full HP, full slots, Hollowed cleared, exhaustion gone, restocked at Wend's and
  Selma's. The "wrecked party" that motivated the cut isn't the party that shows up here, and two
  flyers make the Ready-action lesson bite harder than one. Your call at the table; one peryton is
  the safe default.
- **This is the flex item:** if the RP runs long — it should — the council and/or the fight slide
  whole into session 6's cold open with nothing lost.
