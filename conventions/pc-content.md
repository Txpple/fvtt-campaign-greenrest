# Read the PC's canon before touching anything tied to a PC

**Rule:** before any PC-touching content (items, trinkets, holy symbols, art, descriptions, build
advice), read:

1. the PC's page in the **Player Backstories** journal (`search-journals` on the PC's name),
2. their sheet (`manage-actors` `get`, plus background and feature items),
3. the PC's entry in `plot/plot.md` (the party, and their campaign ties).

Weave in canon details (names, places, numbers), and never invent a fact the backstory already
answers. If it doesn't answer it, ask the DM.

**Why:** a paladin's holy symbol flavoured for the wrong god by inference, or the wrong oath
recommended, when the backstory page names both outright, is exactly the error this prevents. The
DM's expectation: backstories are read carefully, from the journals *and* the character sheets.
