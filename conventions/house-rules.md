# House rules and table conventions

The table's standing rulings: where this table played differently from the 2024 rules, or settled a
question for good. Rulings made mid-session are in that session's `gm-notes-mechanics` (or
`gm-notes.md` for sessions 1–6); the full ledger is `plot/plot.md` §8 ("Rulings ledger") and §9.0
("Table conventions").

## Standing rulings

- **Mundane ammunition is not tracked.** Ordinary arrows and bolts are assumed; only *magic*
  ammunition is a real item with a real count. An empty ammo count on a sheet is not an open item,
  a shopping hook or a bug.
- **Party Camp is the staging scene between sessions.** Where the party's tokens sit on it is not
  where the party is in the fiction. Between sessions the tokens wait there with the scene active,
  and the first move of a session is to put them back where the story left off. A staging position
  is never a location.
